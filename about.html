Who we are

Built on trust, one home at a time.

E & B. started as two friends with one small office and a simple idea: moving home should feel exciting, not exhausting. Today we are a team of 18 advisors, but we still treat every move as if it were our own.

We know the streets, the schools, and the neighbours. That local knowledge helps our clients make calm, confident decisions.
